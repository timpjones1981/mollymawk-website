About the band

A mollymawk is one of the smaller albatrosses of the Southern Ocean, the bird sailors watched for from the rail.

Mollymawk the band are Tim Jones (Banned from the Moor, The Dark Lanterns) on guitar and vocals, Maddie May (The Kentish Buccaneers) on accordion and vocals, and Sue McVay (Black Friday, Jack O’Rourke) on fiddle.

We play mostly traditional folk music, but the fun bits. Songs from the land, songs from the sea, and tune sets that we enjoy and that get people dancing. Although this is a new project, you may have seen members of our band playing at Beautiful Days, Broadstairs Folk Week, Sark Folk Festival, Widecombe Fair and many other places.
